About TechBuddy

Empowering Digital Freedom Across the Atlantic

TechBuddy is an independent cybersecurity testing lab and consumer advocacy publication founded to cut through corporate VPN marketing hype with forensic data and legal scrutiny.

Our Mission & Independence

In an era of sweeping surveillance legislation—from the US CLOUD Act to the UK's Investigatory Powers Act—internet freedom is no longer a luxury; it is a fundamental civil necessity. TechBuddy was founded by former network infrastructure engineers and cyber law specialists in London and New York to provide everyday citizens and professionals with objective, reproducible evaluation of virtual private networks and encryption tools.

📡

10Gbps Bare-Metal Labs

We operate dedicated test infrastructure across Tier-3 data centers in London, Ashburn, and New York to measure true throughput and latency.

🔍

DNS & WebRTC Forensics

Every VPN undergoes 48-hour continuous packet inspection using Wireshark to guarantee zero IP, IPv6, or DNS leak vulnerabilities.

⚖️

Jurisdiction Verification

We scrutinize corporate ownership structures, offshore corporate registrations, and independent third-party audits (PwC, KPMG, Deloitte).
